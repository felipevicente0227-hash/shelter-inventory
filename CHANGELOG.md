# What changed in each version

Newest first. To get the latest version, download it from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/felipevicente0227-hash/shelter-inventory/releases/latest) -
your data stays where it is.

## v0.6.1 - 2026-10-04

- Fresh pictures of the app on the project page, showing how it looks today.
- Each download page now says what changed in that version.
- Small fixes.

## v0.6.0 - 2026-09-28

- New **Count sheet**: print a list of every item with a blank box next to
  it, for walking the shelves and counting stock.
- New **Help** page, with an **Email about a problem** button that opens a
  message to the author in your own mail program.

## v0.5.1 - 2026-09-28

- Fixed a window that could open empty on slower computers.

## v0.5.0 - 2026-09-28

- **Import the list you already keep**: open your existing Excel or CSV file
  and the app brings the items in.
- **Use-by dates**: give an item a use-by date and the app warns you when it
  has expired or is about to.

## v0.4.0 - 2026-09-03

- New dashboard-style window that shows what you have and what needs
  attention at a glance.
- The old window is kept and opens automatically on computers that cannot
  show the new one.

## v0.2.0 - 2026-09-02

- First public release.
- Safe saving, with automatic backups of your data.
- Edit, delete and undo.
- A ready-to-share **"What we need"** list for donors.
